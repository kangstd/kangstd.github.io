Research interests

  • Federated learning: client sampling, partial participation, server-side state
  • Staleness-aware refresh scheduling and tail control under a fixed per-round budget
  • Feedback coupling between adaptive aggregation and client selection

About

I am a first-year direct-entry Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering at Beihang University. My current work studies how adaptive aggregation weights in federated learning become the next round's sampling distribution, and how the resulting persistent server-side cache accumulates staleness. I am interested in schedulers that bound the worst-case refresh gap without increasing the per-round communication budget.
