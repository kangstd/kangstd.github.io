Research interests

Federated learning

  • Client sampling and partial participation, with persistent server-side state
  • Staleness-aware refresh scheduling and tail control under a fixed per-round budget
  • Feedback coupling between adaptive aggregation and client selection

Multi-agent control

  • Cooperative control of networked multi-agent systems
  • Distributed coordination when agents exchange only partial state over a constrained interaction topology

About

I am a first-year direct-entry Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering at Beihang University. My work has two directions that share one question: how a group of agents should coordinate when none of them can see the whole picture. In federated learning I study how adaptive aggregation weights become the next round's sampling distribution, and how the resulting persistent server-side cache accumulates staleness; in multi-agent control I study cooperative coordination over limited interaction topologies.

On the federated-learning side, I am interested in schedulers that bound the worst-case refresh gap without increasing the per-round communication budget.
